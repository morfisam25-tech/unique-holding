03 / Review & Qualification

Commercial review starts with the requirement.

The inquiry is reviewed rather than automatically matched or quoted. Published reference information can support initial review, but it does not determine the final offered-lot specification.

Requirement

Buyer parameters

Product, grade or target specification, quantity, destination, timing and technical requirement are reviewed for commercial context.

Qualification

Commercial route review

Relevant commercial options are reviewed against the requested requirement. The site does not represent this step as automatic sourcing or a promise that a commercial option exists.

Technical context

Reference versus offered lot

Published reference information supports initial review. Final offered-lot specification and commercial terms are confirmed during commercial review.

04 / Offered-Lot Confirmation

Standing catalog facts stop where transaction-specific review begins.

When appropriate to the transaction, producer, origin, packing, final specification, availability and commercial terms are confirmed against the offered lot. They are not represented here as standing catalog guarantees.

Commercial boundary

An RFQ is a request for commercial review. Preparing or sending the email does not itself confirm stock, supply, producer, origin, specification, acceptance or commercial terms.
