02 / RFQ Fields

What to include.

These seven fields give Industrial Sales the minimum commercial context needed to review the inquiry properly.

01

Product

Full product name or accepted abbreviation.

02

Grade

Required grade, form, concentration or commercial variant.

03

Quantity

Required quantity and, where relevant, delivery schedule.

04

Destination

Country, port, city or delivery point for commercial and logistics review.

05

Timing

Required shipment or delivery window.

06

Specification

Target specification, TDS, SDS or buyer parameters where available.

07

Contact

Company, contact name, email and telephone details.

Offer basis

Confirmation

Final specification, packing, availability and commercial terms are confirmed against the offered lot.

03 / Commercial Process

What happens after the RFQ.

The inquiry moves through commercial review rather than an automated price list.

01

Requirement Review

Product, grade, specification, quantity, destination and timing are checked for completeness.

02

Offer Review

Available commercial routes are reviewed against the requested specification and transaction requirements.

03

Terms & Documentation

Applicable specification, packing, documentation and commercial terms are confirmed for the offered lot.

04

Logistics & Delivery

The agreed commercial and logistics path is coordinated through delivery and handover.
