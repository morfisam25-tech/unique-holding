01 / Overview

A specialist intelligence business with a distinct operating identity.

Evidence Axis focuses on competitive discovery, evidence verification and decision support for B2B SaaS founders, executives and product or strategy teams.

Evidence before interpretation.

Part of the Unique Holding technology portfolio.

Evidence Axis is a specialist venture within the Unique Holding portfolio. It maintains its own public brand, specialist website, research identity and direct market-facing presence.

Focus

Competitive Intelligence

Research into competitors, market movement and public signals relevant to a business decision.

Research discipline

Evidence Verification

Claims are tested against source material and uncertainty remains visible where the evidence does not establish an answer.

Output

Decision Support

Verified research is organized into concise material intended to support a defined strategic or competitive decision.
