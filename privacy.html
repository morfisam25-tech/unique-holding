This notice describes how the Unique Holding corporate website currently handles personal information, browser storage and technical website requests.

Back to Unique Holding ↗
01 / Website Interaction

What this website currently collects directly.

The website does not provide user accounts, registration, an on-site contact form or an automated quotation form. Contact, telephone and RFQ links open the visitor’s own email or telephone application.

Direct website collection

No account or web-form submission

Personal information is not submitted into a Unique Holding website database through the public pages currently published.

Email & telephone

Business correspondence

If you choose to email or call a published contact, the information you provide is handled as ordinary business correspondence through the communication service you use.

RFQ route

Email-based inquiry

The industrial RFQ page prepares a structured email in the visitor’s own email application; it does not transmit the RFQ through a website backend.

02 / Cookies & Storage

No analytics or advertising cookies are implemented by the site code.

The site’s own JavaScript is used for navigation and the email-based RFQ helper. It does not set first-party cookies and does not use localStorage or sessionStorage. No analytics platform, advertising pixel or behavioural tracking script is currently implemented.

Cookie banner

Because the current site code does not set non-essential first-party cookies or run analytics or advertising tracking, the website does not currently display a cookie-consent banner.

Future changes

If analytics, embedded services or other tracking technologies are introduced, this notice and any required visitor controls should be updated before those technologies are deployed publicly.

03 / Technical Delivery

Standard technical requests may still occur.

Website hosting infrastructure may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser information, requested page and request time. Some current pages also load externally hosted photography, which causes the visitor’s browser to request those image files from the relevant external host.

Hosting

Technical request data

Infrastructure providers may process ordinary connection and security data required to serve the website.

External resources

Hosted imagery

External image hosts process the browser request used to retrieve their files under their own technical and privacy practices.

External links

Other websites

When you follow a link to another website, that destination applies its own privacy and cookie practices.
