02 / Current Website Data Flows

The public site is informational and uses direct contact handoffs.

The current website has no account-registration flow and no general server-backed contact form. Most routes present corporate, industrial, technology or portfolio information and link visitors to the relevant contact destination.

BROWSING

Public pages

Visiting a public route causes the browser to request the page and its required assets. Ordinary connection/request metadata can therefore be handled by the network and hosting infrastructure that delivers the site.

INDUSTRIAL RFQ

Browser-prepared email

The Industrial Sales builder accepts requirement and contact fields in the browser and prepares a mailto draft. The website does not send that RFQ to a server itself.

DIRECT ROUTES

Email and telephone

Corporate and sales email links use mailto. Telephone links use tel. Following those links hands communication to the visitor’s chosen email or telephone service.

03 / Direct Business Correspondence

Website handoff and business correspondence are different stages.

A mailto or tel link does not by itself mean that the static website stored a submitted message. If you actually send an email, make a call or otherwise provide information through the selected communication service, that information can become business correspondence outside the static web page.

WHAT YOU MAY CHOOSE TO SEND

Depending on the route and what you provide, correspondence may include your name, company or organization, email address, telephone number, commercial inquiry content, product/grade/quantity/destination/timing information, technical or specification information and additional notes.

LIMITED BUSINESS PURPOSES

Such correspondence may be used to respond to the communication, review an industrial requirement or continue the corporate, technology or venture discussion requested by the sender. This factual description is not a statement of a specific KVKK legal basis.

04 / Cookies / Browser Storage

No website-set cookie or browser-storage item was observed in the reviewed flows.

During the Phase 17 browser review of all 16 public routes on 1 September 2026, the current implementation did not set cookies in the reviewed flows and did not create localStorage keys, sessionStorage keys, IndexedDB databases or service-worker registrations. No analytics or advertising request and no page-script XHR, fetch or beacon call was observed.

COOKIE-BANNER DECISION

No non-essential cookie-choice banner was added because the reviewed implementation did not expose a non-essential cookie or similar tracking mechanism. This is a statement about the reviewed implementation, not a promise that the website can never change.

05 / Technical Delivery / External Requests

Site delivery can involve ordinary request metadata and specific external image requests.

Network and hosting infrastructure can receive ordinary information needed to deliver a web request, such as an IP address, browser/request information, the requested resource and request time. This notice does not state an exact infrastructure-provider role, log-retention period, processing location or transfer mechanism because those matters have not been source-locked for publication.

EXTERNAL IMAGE HOST

images.unsplash.com

The reviewed current implementation requested externally hosted image files from images.unsplash.com on the Home, Energy & Trade, Products and Industrial Sales routes. The Privacy and Legal routes do not request that external hero photography after this Phase 17 revision.

NOT OBSERVED

Third-party active code

No third-party script, remote font, third-party embed, external media stream, analytics request, advertising request, XHR, fetch or beacon was observed in the reviewed website flows.

EXTERNAL RESOURCE PRACTICES

Host-specific handling

When the browser directly requests an externally hosted image, the destination host receives the technical request needed to return that file and applies its own technical and privacy practices.

06 / External Destinations

External links become separate destinations when you choose to follow them.

The website links to destinations such as the Evidence Axis specialist website/contact route and Google Maps. Those destinations are not presented as embedded services on this notice. Once followed, their operators apply their own content, technical and privacy practices.
