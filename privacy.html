01 / Direct Contact

Information you choose to provide.

The website does not use account registration or on-site submission forms. Contact, telephone and industrial RFQ links open the visitor’s own email or telephone application.

Email & telephone

Business correspondence

If you choose to email or call a published contact, the information you provide becomes part of that direct business correspondence.

Industrial RFQ

Email-based inquiry

The industrial RFQ route prepares a structured email in the visitor’s own email application for direct delivery to Industrial Sales.

Communication services

Your chosen provider

Email and telephone communications also operate under the technical and privacy practices of the communication service you use.

02 / Cookies & Tracking

Analytics and advertising cookies are not currently used.

The website does not currently use analytics cookies, advertising pixels or similar behavioural tracking. It also does not store website data in localStorage or sessionStorage.

Cookie banner

No non-essential cookie-consent banner is currently shown because analytics and advertising cookies are not in use on the website.

Changes to this notice

If analytics, embedded services or other tracking technologies are introduced, this notice and any required visitor controls should be updated accordingly.

03 / Technical Delivery

Standard technical requests may still occur.

Website hosting infrastructure may process standard request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address, browser information, requested page and request time. Some pages also load externally hosted photography, which requires the visitor’s browser to request those image files from the relevant external host.

Hosting

Technical request data

Infrastructure providers may process ordinary connection and security data required to serve the website.

External resources

Hosted imagery

External image hosts process the browser request used to retrieve their files under their own technical and privacy practices.

External links

Other websites

When you follow a link to another website, that destination applies its own privacy and cookie practices.
