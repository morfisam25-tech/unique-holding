01 / Overview

Commercial capability across industrial supply.

From Istanbul, the industrial business handles buyer requirements across import, domestic supply, export and re-export routes for petrochemical and chemical materials.

From requirement to delivery.

Each inquiry starts with the material, grade, quantity, destination and timing. The commercial process then covers offer review, specification confirmation, documentation, logistics coordination and delivery under agreed terms.

Commercial coordination across physical trade.

Industrial transactions require aligned documentation, logistics, timing and handover. The operating role is centered on coordinating those commercial steps around the confirmed transaction.

Execution around the transaction.
