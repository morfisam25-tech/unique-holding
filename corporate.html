Corporate

A group built from operating businesses.

Unique Holding is based in Istanbul, Türkiye. Its current operating foundation is industrial and petrochemical trade, with specialist ventures developed alongside the core business.

Group Structure

Parent group.
Distinct operating fields.

The group structure separates industrial trading activity from specialist ventures while keeping a clear corporate identity at group level.

Industrial trade

The group’s industrial activity covers import, domestic supply, export and re-export of petrochemical and industrial chemical materials, subject to availability, offered-lot specifications and agreed commercial terms.

Market intelligence

The trading activity has historically used paid specialist petrochemical market intelligence services, including S&P Global Platts.

Group ventures

Evidence Axis is the group’s current specialist competitive-intelligence venture, focused on evidence-backed decision support for founders, product teams and executives in fast-moving B2B markets.
