01 / Who We Are

A group structure built around real operating activity.

Unique Holding provides one corporate identity for distinct business worlds: industrial trade and energy on one side, technology and intelligence on the other.

Industrial trade as the operating foundation.

Turkish corporate operations support import, domestic supply, export and re-export of industrial materials, including petrochemicals and chemicals. Technology and intelligence activities are developed as separate specialist businesses and ventures within the broader group context.

02 / Group Structure

Clear operating identities.

03 / History

A concise operating timeline.

Public milestones are intentionally limited to points that can be supported. Detailed commercial records remain internal.

2020

Turkish corporate operations.

Corporate activity in Türkiye established the operating base for industrial trading and commercial supply.

2021–2023

Industrial trading expanded.

The business developed customer relationships and cross-border commercial activity in chemicals and petrochemical materials.

2023

Substantial commercial activity.

Company records document a period of significant industrial sales and international incoming commercial funds. Exact figures are not published on this public site without a dedicated accounting and documentary review.

2026

Technology and intelligence platform.

Evidence Axis and new digital ventures expanded the group’s public activity beyond industrial trade into competitive intelligence and venture development.
